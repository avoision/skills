A converted factory turned duplex loft, with a private outdoor patio, four proper double beds, and four bathrooms — the best bathroom-to-bedroom ratio in this entire report. Located in Charenton-le-Pont, a safe, leafy suburb one metro stop beyond Paris's edge, it's the only 4-bedroom listing within budget. Line 8 from the nearby Charenton-Écoles station reaches Bastille in 13 minutes and Opéra in about 20. The trade-off is clear: you're not in Paris proper, which means no wandering out to a boulangerie or spontaneous Marais café stops. But if your group plans to use the apartment as a home base for day trips rather than wanting to step out into the city at every turn, this is exceptional value — near Bois de Vincennes for morning runs, total quiet at night, and a genuinely striking living space. Best for groups who want comfort and space over address.
